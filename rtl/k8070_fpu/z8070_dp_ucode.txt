# z8070_dp_ucode -- the Data Processor microprogram, one word per CLK.D clock.
#
# The source of the generated ROM in gen/. Only
# the add/subtract, multiply and divide totals are published (1985 Data
# Book p.532); every split of a total into steps, every other length and
# every short path is a guess, tagged on each routine.
#
# Records, whitespace separated, '#' starts a comment:
#
#   act     <NAME> <what the datapath does>
#   routine <name> <published|guess> <note...>
#   word    <label> <ACT> <seq> [<target>] [end]
#             seq NEXT (next word), JMP (to target), JCOND (to target if
#             the action's test holds: a special operand in a special
#             clock, a zero sum in LZC, a zero remainder in CORR, more
#             steps to go in a loop step, an integer result in NORM, a
#             decimal store in IRND, anything else in QSEL; else next), RET (to the body of the latched
#             operation); end: the operation's last clock
#   entry   <kind hex> <entry label> <body label> <name>
#
# Words take addresses in file order. A kind with no entry runs the tail.

# ---- datapath actions --------------------------------------------------
act UNPA  operand bus to A, classify (exponent field, integer and quiet bits)
act RENA  A: leading-zero count, left shift, exponent minus count; DE; zero significand to zero
act UNPB  operand bus or a made zero to B, classify, sign flip for a subtract (FABS/FNEG/FCLR: the operand as it is)
act RENB  B: as RENA
act SPEC  add special operands (NaN, infinity, both zero) resolved into S with flags
act EXPD  exponent adder: ea - eb
act SWAP  larger exponent to A (a zero operand loses), exponent adder: -d if swapped; effective subtract
act ALGN  shifter: B significand right by min(d, 67) into S, sticky collected
act ADD   adder: A significand plus or minus S
act NEG   adder: 0 - S and sign flip if the subtract went negative
act CRY   shifter: S right one with sticky on a carry out; exponent adder: +1
act LZC   leading-zero count of S; a zero sum becomes the signed zero of the rounding mode
act NORM  shifter: S left by the count; exponent adder: minus the count; the denormalize amount (subnormal scale - exponent)
act DNRM  tininess; shifter: denormalize a tiny result by the amount formed at NORM
act RND   precision fold into guard and sticky, round decision, adder: increment at the precision LSB
act RNG   zero, overflow (infinity or largest finite) and storage form; INX, UN, FOV
act PACK  shifter: significand left for storage; result latch loaded
act WB    result handed to the register file and the Interface Processor
act SPCC  compare: NaN operand to unordered, signaling NaN to NAN
act SIGC  adder: A significand - B significand; magnitude relation: exponent, else the borrow and zero
act SREL  signed relation: zeros equal, signs, both negative reversed
act CMAP  compare code (Table 1); unordered on an X compare: INV, reason 4
act SOP   sign unit: B to S with the sign cleared (FABS) or flipped (FNEG), or Normal Zero (FCLR)
act MSPC  multiply special operands (NaN, zero times infinity, infinity, zero) resolved into S with flags
act MEXP  exponent adder: ea + eb; product sign; shifter: multiplicand left one into S (the accumulator offset)
act M3    adder: 2M + M into X (3M, shifted left one); shifter: B right to its top n bits, the rest to sticky; exponent adder: minus the bias; S cleared; step count
act MSTP  radix-4 step: S plus 0, M, 2M or 3M by B[1:0], shifted right two, the two low bits into B shifted right two
act PRD   product into S: adder adds M for the double's integer bit; guard, round and sticky from B
act DSPC  divide special operands (NaN, 0/0, inf/inf, infinity, zero divisor) resolved into S with flags
act DEXP  exponent adder: ea - eb; quotient sign
act DLD   exponent adder: plus the bias and the precision offset; shifter: dividend right two (double) or three into S; X, QM cleared; step count
act DSTP  SRT step: digit from S[67:62] and D[62:60]; adder: 4w minus or plus D or 2D; X takes a positive digit, QM a negative one
act QSEL  adder: quotient X - QM, less one on a negative remainder, into S; the remainder's sticky below it (BCD store: the fraction alone, IX on a quotient of one or more)
act RENS  remainder: A renormalized into S; A kept as stored
act RSPC  remainder special operands (NaN, infinite dividend or zero divisor, infinite divisor, zero dividend) with flags
act RLEN  batch length: n - 1, n odd, complete, step count
act RLD   exponent adder: e - (n - 1); shifter: dividend right two or three; D to A; X, QM cleared
act CORR  remainder: zero ends with a signed zero; negative: adder w + D; remainder flags
act SSPC  square root special operands (NaN, zero, negative, infinity) with flags
act SEXP  exponent adder: (eb + bias + twice the precision offset) / 2, parity
act SLD   shifter: radicand fraction into A; first root bit: root 1, remainder 0 or 1 + f1; step count
act SSTP  root step: adder 4 rem + pair - (4 root + 1), or + (4 root + 3) after a negative remainder, root bit into B; shifter: radicand left two
act SRES  root into S (shifter: B right one), the extended's leading bit, sticky from the remainder (a negative one exact at -(2 root + 1)) and the radicand left
act FSPC  FINT special operands (NaN, infinity, zero) with flags; exponent adder: shift count bias + 63 - eb
act IALN  shifter: B right to the integer point into S, sticky collected (none when already an integer: FINT keeps the scale, a conversion overflows)
act IRND  round S at its LSB (the integer point): the round clock with the extended fold
act ILD   operand bus integer through the adder: 0 - v when negative into S, the sign, exponent bias + 63
act XSPC  conversion special operands: NaN (INV, reason 7), infinity (IX), zero; the indefinite integer or the zero container
act IFIN  adder: 0 - S when negative; range by width; the indefinite on overflow; IX or INX
act BSPC  BCD digit check (a non-decimal digit: QNaN, INV, reason 6); exponent bias + 63; the sign; digit count
act BSTP  BCD digit: adder 8 acc + 2 acc + digit (low bits and carry-in) into S; shifter: the digits left four in B
act BLD   shifter: integer right three into S (the dividend / 8); B the divisor 10^19; X, QM cleared; step count
act BDIG  decimal digit: adder 8 g + 2 g, the integer part a digit; shifter: digits left four in B, the digit in
act BOUT  digits (B) to S with the sign; IX or INX
act NOJOB unused address: no work, the operation ends

# ---- shared: unpack two operands ---------------------------------------
routine unpack guess two operands in successive clocks over one bus, each classified then renormalized
word UNP_A   UNPA NEXT
word UNP_AR  RENA NEXT
word UNP_B   UNPB NEXT
word UNP_BR  RENB RET

# ---- add, subtract, reverse subtract, load -----------------------------
routine add published 18 clocks every precision (the total); the split is a guess; 7 on a special operand and 14 on an exact cancellation are guesses
word ADD_SP  SPEC JCOND TAIL
word ADD_ED  EXPD NEXT
word ADD_SW  SWAP NEXT
word ADD_AL  ALGN NEXT
word ADD_AD  ADD  NEXT
word ADD_NG  NEG  NEXT
word ADD_CY  CRY  JMP ADD_LZ

# ---- BCD load ----------------------------------------------------------
routine bcdload guess 27 clocks (24 for a zero, 4 for a non-decimal digit): the container read, the digit check, one x10 shift-add per digit (19), the shared tail without DNRM
word BCL_LD  UNPB NEXT
word BCL_SP  BSPC JCOND TAIL
word BCL_IT  BSTP JCOND BCL_IT

# ---- normalize, round, range (add, multiply, divide, remainder, square root, conversions)
routine round guess the add path's last five clocks; an integer result is never tiny and skips DNRM
word ADD_LZ  LZC  JCOND TAIL
word ADD_NM  NORM JCOND ADD_RD
word ADD_DN  DNRM NEXT
word ADD_RD  RND  NEXT
word ADD_RG  RNG  JMP TAIL

# ---- compare -----------------------------------------------------------
routine compare guess 11 clocks: the unpack, a magnitude compare on the adders, the relation and code
word CMP_SP  SPCC NEXT
word CMP_ED  EXPD NEXT
word CMP_SG  SIGC NEXT
word CMP_SR  SREL NEXT
word CMP_CM  CMAP JMP TAIL

# ---- FABS, FNEG, FCLR --------------------------------------------------
routine sign guess 4 clocks: quiet bit operations, the operand passes unchanged but for the sign
word SGN_LD  UNPB  NEXT
word SGN_OP  SOP   JMP TAIL

# ---- FMUL, FPWR2 -------------------------------------------------------
routine mul published 28/42/48 clocks (16 + floor(nS/2) radix-4 steps); the split is a guess; 7 on a special operand is a guess
word MUL_SP  MSPC JCOND TAIL
word MUL_EX  MEXP NEXT
word MUL_3M  M3   NEXT
word MUL_IT  MSTP JCOND MUL_IT
word MUL_PR  PRD  JMP ADD_CY

# ---- FDIV, FRDIV -------------------------------------------------------
routine div published 29/43/49 clocks (17 + floor(nS/2), floor(nS/2) + 2 SRT digits); the split is a guess; 7 on a special operand is a guess
word DIV_SP  DSPC JCOND TAIL
word DIV_EX  DEXP NEXT
word DIV_LD  DLD  NEXT
word DIV_IT  DSTP JCOND DIV_IT
word DIV_QS  QSEL JCOND ADD_LZ

# ---- BCD store ---------------------------------------------------------
routine bcdstore guess 63 clocks (5 on a special operand): the conversion front end (5), h / 10^19 on the divide's 34 SRT digits and QSEL, one x10 per digit (19), the digits out, the tail
word BCS_DG  BDIG JCOND BCS_DG
word BCS_OU  BOUT JMP TAIL

# ---- FREM, FREMSTEP ----------------------------------------------------
routine rem guess 17 + floor(n/2) clocks for n quotient bits (29/43/49 a full batch), 12 + floor(n/2) on a zero remainder, 7 on a special operand
word REM_A   UNPA NEXT
word REM_AR  RENS JMP UNP_B
word REM_SP  RSPC JCOND TAIL
word REM_ED  EXPD NEXT
word REM_LN  RLEN NEXT
word REM_LD  RLD  NEXT
word REM_IT  DSTP JCOND REM_IT
word REM_CR  CORR JCOND TAIL
word REM_LZ  LZC  JMP ADD_NM

# ---- FSQR --------------------------------------------------------------
routine sqrt guess 13 + nS clocks (37/66/77), one root bit a clock; 5 on a special operand; the shared tail: LZC's count is fixed by the precision, DNRM shifts only a single or double root of a tiny extended operand
word SQR_SP  SSPC JCOND TAIL
word SQR_EX  SEXP NEXT
word SQR_LD  SLD  NEXT
word SQR_IT  SSTP JCOND SQR_IT
word SQR_RS  SRES JMP ADD_LZ

# ---- FINT --------------------------------------------------------------
routine fint guess 11 clocks (8 for a zero integer, 5 on a special operand): the unpack's B half, special select, integer align and round, the shared tail without DNRM
word FIN_SP  FSPC JCOND TAIL
word FIN_AL  IALN NEXT
word FIN_RD  IRND JMP ADD_LZ

# ---- integer to float --------------------------------------------------
routine itof guess 7 clocks (4 for a zero): one adder pass reads the integer, the shared tail without DNRM
word I2F_LD  ILD  JMP ADD_LZ

# ---- float to integer, BCD store front end -----------------------------
routine ftoi guess 8 clocks (5 on a special operand): the unpack's B half, special select, integer align and round, negate and range
word CVI_SP  XSPC JCOND TAIL
word CVI_AL  IALN NEXT
word CVI_RD  IRND JCOND BCS_LD
word FIX_FN  IFIN JMP TAIL
word BCS_LD  BLD  JMP DIV_IT

# ---- shared tail -------------------------------------------------------
routine tail guess result latch, then the hand-over
word TAIL    PACK NEXT
word TAIL_WB WB   NEXT end

# ---- dispatch ----------------------------------------------------------
entry 00 UNP_A  ADD_SP FADD
entry 01 UNP_A  ADD_SP FSUB
entry 02 UNP_A  ADD_SP FRVSUB
entry 03 UNP_A  MUL_SP FMUL
entry 04 UNP_A  DIV_SP FDIV
entry 05 UNP_A  DIV_SP FRDIV
entry 06 UNP_A  MUL_SP FPWR2
entry 07 UNP_B  SQR_SP FSQR
entry 08 UNP_A  ADD_SP FLD
entry 09 SGN_LD SGN_LD FABS
entry 0A SGN_LD SGN_LD FNEG
entry 0B SGN_LD SGN_LD FCLR
entry 0C UNP_B  FIN_SP FINT
entry 0D UNP_A  CMP_SP FCP
entry 0E UNP_A  CMP_SP FCPX
entry 0F UNP_A  CMP_SP FCPZ
entry 10 UNP_A  CMP_SP FCPZX
entry 11 REM_A  REM_SP FREM
entry 12 REM_A  REM_SP FREMSTEP
entry 20 I2F_LD I2F_LD ITOF
entry 21 UNP_B  CVI_SP FTOI
entry 22 BCL_LD BCL_LD BCDLD
entry 23 UNP_B  CVI_SP BCDST
